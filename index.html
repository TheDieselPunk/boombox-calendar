Miami venue calendars

One subscribable feed per venue, rebuilt from Shotgun, Dice and Edmtrain every 6 hours.

How to subscribe
  1. Click Copy & open Google Calendar on a venue (desktop browser — the phone app can't add calendars by URL).
  2. Google's From URL form opens in a new tab: paste, then Add calendar.
Google refreshes subscribed feeds on its own schedule (typically 12–24 h). Shotgun- and Dice-listed events carry real start/end times; where a listing has no time yet (Edmtrain only), the event uses the venue's usual hours and says so in its description. Google's one-click cid= subscribe link is currently broken on their side, hence the copy-and-paste.
